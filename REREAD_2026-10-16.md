# Re-read of the history documents (2026-10-16)

Every .md history file (STAGE0-STAGE6, TRACKER, CLEANUP, BFS, LESSONS, STATUS) was re-read for conclusions that a defect or a process confound present at the time may have produced, against the list of defects fixed since (LESSONS.md, BFS.md 8). Ten readers listed candidates; the candidates were merged into themes and each theme was verified adversarially, by re-reading the cited lines and code and, where cheap, by re-running the measurement (GL tools in a scratch environment that reproduced every documented number exactly; the 20261010n logs and records of the branch `results`).

Verdicts: 41 overturned, 45 need a re-test, 23 stand, 14 were already corrected in the documents, 3 were not real concerns (high importance: 10 overturned, 7 to re-test). One theme was verified twice (sections 3 and 4); duplicates are marked. Paths marked (session scratch) were working files of that session and are not kept. LESSONS.md section 21 holds the lessons drawn from this re-read; this file is the item-by-item record.

## The cross-cutting findings

1. **No endpoint ever cited as a minimum or as stationary was one.** Every such endpoint ended on budget-limited rounds (code 4), on premature code-0 exits of `--sqp-stat rel`, or at a fixed point of code later found defective. The "two SISO local minima" of the BFS (554.97, 598.11) admit certified descent steps of 0.91 % and 0.83 %; the "stationary kink" 6.2e-4 of GL KSP+B+C is an artefact of normalising by the MEAN branch-gradient norm (rows 44 and 1734: the min-norm element ignores the large row, the mean is dominated by it); the "145.586 fixed point" basin reached 123.90 in 20261010n. The multimodality chain ("multistart over controllers, drop position optimisation") rests on no demonstrated distinct minimum, and positions were never restarted from elsewhere.
2. **`--sqp-stat rel` is not a stationarity test**: code 0 fired after 1 iteration while the next round gained 2.4-5.4 % (GL) and while a certified 0.8-0.9 % step existed (BFS SISO). A latent bug since the first commit (`run_case_epi.py`, `int(stats.get('termination_code') or -1) == 0` reads code 0 as -1) means "exchange converged" never fired, which protected the runs from that test.
3. **Stationarity measures were badly calibrated**: the one-branch measures (check_optimality, the --dump residual, check_kkt) are not the condition and disagree with each other; kkt_residual depends strongly on the active-set tolerance; any residual must be normalised consistently (smallest or multiplier-weighted row, and the measured first-order decrease in gamma units), never by the mean row norm.
4. **The cost premise "the inner solve is not the bottleneck" is false on the BFS** (evaluations and level set > 90 % of the wall; even GL ne=200 spent 79 % in the SQP). So "do not add another solver class" has lost its justification on the BFS, though no alternative has been measured.
5. **Small-scale and reduced-model measurements were extrapolated too far**: winding counts run at 15-21 % efficiency on 48 ranks (one segment sets the wall); the full mesh needs about 25 GB per rank for the lattice (about 20 ranks per node; codesign.slurm's defaults cannot hold it); the full mesh was never optimised or plant-certified.
6. **Path noise is large and was never measured on the BFS**: a 1e-12 start perturbation gives 19.8 % mid-run and 0.014 % at the stop on GL SISO, 9 % mid-run and 0.7 % at the stop on GL MIMO. The 1 % A/B rule needs a BFS A/A first; the 20261010n strip D moved by 1-3 points within hours (c177 ended +1.07 % for the strip).
7. **New defects found**: the BFS QP failures are an ill-conditioned within-round BFGS matrix (condition about 5e19) defeating Clarabel's default equilibration (bounded equilibration solves both replayed failures), not the rows; the trust radius is 100-300x the accepted step and 37-40 of 40 QP steps sit on its boundary, so step control is done by the line search alone; `--gate-prefix` cannot find a prefix where one was needed; `--sqp-row-res-tol` acts only on the reduced path; tools/kkt_residual.py never sets alpha_argmax; tools/check_invariants.sh hard-codes the frozen literal of another environment.

**Verifier's summary.**

The main pattern: the GL-era SQP rules were calibrated on stalls or on single replays, and the BFS records now contradict two of them outright.

Scripts: (session scratch) adv_sqp/replay_qp.py and replay2.py.

New defects to add to the seed list:
